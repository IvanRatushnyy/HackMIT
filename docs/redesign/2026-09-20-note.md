# elute front end, redesign note

20 September 2026. Every screen rebuilt on one material; the data, the label rules, the routes and the
persisted call are unchanged. Screenshots of the result are in `shots/after/`, of the previous build in
`shots/before/`.

## Direction

Three passes in one day, and the third is the one that shipped.

1. **Paper cards on a grey ground.** Competent and generic: the SaaS-card kit, and an ink startup screen that
   cut to a light page. Rejected in review.
2. **A reprint, no cards.** One paper page, blocks under ink rules. Coherent, but it read as white and flat, and
   it lost the colour and the arrival motion the team liked.
3. **Glass on a living ground.** Under every page a slow mesh gradient in the brand's tints (Paper's
   `@paper-design/shaders-react`, MIT, WebGL), still under reduced motion. Every reading surface is a glass sheet
   over it. The shard band is back in the header on every page, composing once per load. The startup screen is
   glass over the same ground, so it dissolves into Entry instead of cutting to it.

Then a fourth pass for cohesion, after review: the shard band and the sharp controls, which belonged to the
first language, went; the header became a glass bar; every control took one soft radius; the ground carries
the colour. The four typefaces became two from Google Fonts: Lusitana for the
wordmark, titles and numerals, Instrument Sans for everything else (the audition in `type-specimen.html`
records the candidates that were tried). And the ledger was slowed and refocused: 26 seconds for ten steps,
the current step alone in focus with its records arriving one by one, finished steps as single lines above
it, nothing shown ahead.

What survives from every pass: the pathway drawn in SVG and revealed action by action; the objections
entering and leaving with the evidence date; the five-box prerequisite walkthrough; the results row that
opens into the four questions.

Libraries considered for the ground and the glass, all on GitHub: Paper shaders (chosen: small, typed,
`MeshGradient` with colour stops, a `speed` prop for reduced motion); react-bits (Aurora, Silk and similar
backgrounds, heavier and component-styled); shadergradient (Three.js, larger); liquid-glass-react and the
vendored liquid-glass-js (refraction over a page snapshot, set aside for the same reason as before: one
snapshot, no live change beneath). Glass itself is CSS: `backdrop-filter`, an inner hairline of light, a tinted
shadow, and a solid fallback under `prefers-reduced-transparency`.

## Tokens

Paper `#fbfcfe`, ink `#06070e`, slate `#47667d`, dust `#d3d3d3`, raspberry `#82204a`, each accent in three
OKLCH steps (deep, mid, tint). Contrast on paper: ink 19.6:1, raspberry 9.1:1, slate 5.9:1, muted text 5.4:1.
Type scale unchanged (12/14/16/20/28/40, line heights on the 8px grid) plus 80px for the Detail title and
19/28 for reading text. Radius 0 on controls; sheets at 16px; the two circles in the ask box.

## The ladder, per screen

- **Entry.** Rung 0: the field, three example chips (12 words). Rung 1: paste a paper under the field, the
  extraction shown before anything runs.
- **Working.** Rung 0: a strip of ten marks, rows arriving as question, source chip, count and first record;
  the status line. Rung 1: a finished step opens beside the ledger.
- **Results.** Rung 0: two registers with their rules printed once; rows under 25 words: class, name, four
  answer cells. Rung 1: the row opens into a four-step walk (does it work in people, could it work, what could
  go wrong, what would it take) with arrow keys. The board by stage remains.
- **Detail.** Rung 0 (about 110 words): name, class, mechanism, best evidence, the drivers, the evidence date,
  four objections, the drawing, the safety flag, the count of unresolved prerequisites. Rung 1: an objection's
  evidence; an action's claim and its sources for and against; a prerequisite's note; the Reactome plate
  enlarged.
- **Sources.** The ledger with every record; a row opens to what was consulted; tool calls, packages and the
  status rules on secondary tabs.
- **Export.** Format and includes beside a preview typeset like the page.

## Formats kept and dropped

Kept: expand in place (objections, ledger rows, results rows), the stepper (the four questions, the five
prerequisites), the date scrubber on Detail (objections enter and leave as the date crosses their publication),
the enlarging plate. Dropped: carousels (nothing on these pages is a set of parallel items where one at a time
loses nothing), popovers for citations (a source line with its link and ledger citation in place is one fewer
layer), sheets (the export is its own page and prints from it).

## Verification

- `npm test`: 40 tests, 6 files, passing, including the nilotinib backtest and the new register rules.
- `npx tsc -b`: clean. `npm run build`: 576 kB of JS (180 kB gzipped; the shader library and Motion are the
  additions, cytoscape is gone), 48 kB of CSS, and 1.6 MB of fonts, of which Newsreader is 950 kB across two
  files and should be subset before a public deploy.
- Screenshots at 1440 wide, real-time waits through `shot-live.mjs` (headless Chrome's virtual time freezes
  compositor animations, so the earlier `shot.sh` is for static shots only): Entry, the splash mid-way, Entry
  with paste, Entry under reduced motion, Working mid-run, Results, the board, metformin drug-first, Detail
  today and at Nov 2017, Detail for a record without a drawing, Sources, Export.
- The backtest on screen: Nov 2017 shows three objections and the clinical link contested; today shows four
  and refuted; Jul 2016 two. `4 of 5 unresolved` today.
- Words: `·` at most once per line in interface text; no em-dash in text the tool writes (record text keeps
  its punctuation); no adjective of degree in interface text.

  it.` (explains the page)
