## Design Context

Full specification, tokens, and file layout: see `DESIGN.md` and `design/`.

### Users
- The accountable expert: a translational scientist or physician-scientist in pharma. Deep domain expertise, time-poor, skeptical of AI by default, professionally on the hook for being wrong, not a command-line user. Knows more biology than the model and wants their own judgment amplified, not replaced. Laptop and desktop; a projector in a demo room.
- The IT/security reviewer: wants to see what the agent actually called, what it depends on, and whether anything is inappropriate for an enterprise environment.

Job to be done: handed an AI-generated drug-repurposing candidate, see why it might be wrong before why it might be right, find the weakest link in its evidence, and defend or kill it to a colleague in your own words.

### Brand Personality
Quiet, exact, skeptical. Confidence that comes from showing its work. A peer review, not a press release.

### Aesthetic Direction
- Minimalist, light theme. White surfaces, near-black ink, few lines, generous whitespace, asymmetric layouts. A reprint with a careful reader's marks, not a dashboard.
- Slate blue-grey is the only recurring highlight. Dust grey and raspberry are secondary accents; raspberry is reserved for the single-source label, the safety flag, and the ask box's shadow while its field has focus.
- Lusitana (lowercase, one weight, tracking −1%) for the wordmark, titles and numerals. Instrument Sans for everything else. Both from Google Fonts, self-hosted.

- A slow mesh gradient in the brand's tints is the ground under every page; every reading surface is glass over it, controls stay solid with one soft radius, and the header is a glass bar with the wordmark alone.
- The brand mark, the shard, appears as the four bands of the startup column and as the tints of the ground; it is never placed behind text.
- Anti-references: dark "AI lab" dashboards with cyan glow, chat bubbles, sparkle icons, red/amber/green confidence lights, bare percentage scores, gradient text, side-stripe cards, glass on the controls, anything that looks like a pitch deck.

### Grid
Work on an 8px grid. Every spacing value, control height, icon size, line height, and column width is a multiple of 8, taken from `design/tokens/space.css`. Consistency beats cleverness: reuse the scale rather than inventing values.

### Design Principles
1. The counter-case is the headline.
2. Known vs. believed, labeled on every claim. Never a bare number.
3. Doubt reads as rigor: emphasis by weight and placement, not decoration.
4. Show the work; never force it. Every citation lands on the Sources page.
5. Say what is synthetic: on the Sources page, and in every export.
6. Never a recommendation. The scientist decides.
7. The shard is the brand; use it with restraint.
8. One grid, one scale: 8px everywhere.
