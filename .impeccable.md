## Design Context

Full specification, tokens, and file layout: see `DESIGN.md` and `design/`.

### Users
- Emergency management officers who watch the board for weeks and act under pressure for hours. Desktop and ops-room displays, day and night.
- Glaciologists and hydrologists who validate forecasts and want the evidence behind every score.
- Field coordinators checking one site on a phone with poor connectivity.

Job to be done: know which glacial sites are moving toward failure, how much lead time remains, and who is downstream, early enough to evacuate.

### Brand Personality
Quiet, exact, steep. Confidence under pressure. A well-kept instrument, not an alarm panel.

### Aesthetic Direction
- Minimalist, light theme. White surfaces, near-black ink, few lines, generous whitespace, asymmetric layouts.
- Slate blue-grey is the only recurring highlight. Dust grey and raspberry are secondary accents; raspberry is reserved for the highest severity.
- Aujournuit at the Airy width with −2% tracking for big titles only. Supreme with −4% tracking, mostly Regular, for everything else.
- The signature element is the shard composition from the mood board: intersecting curved planes in the four colors. Used as the mark and hero, never behind body text.
- Anti-references: dark dashboards with cyan glow, icons above every heading, traffic-light severity, gradient text, side-stripe cards, weather-app looks.

### Grid
Work on an 8px grid. Every spacing value, control height, icon size, line height, and column width is a multiple of 8, taken from `design/tokens/space.css`. Consistency beats cleverness: reuse the scale rather than inventing values.

### Design Principles
1. Lead time is the headline.
2. Severity by weight and placement, not decoration.
3. Show the evidence behind every score.
4. Quiet by default, so urgency is unmistakable.
5. The shard is the brand; use it with restraint.
6. One grid, one scale: 8px everywhere.
